# Code of Conduct

Pify welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, assume good intent when reasonable, and focus feedback on ideas and work. Give others room to ask questions and disagree.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a problem, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private contact method is available, open a public issue asking to arrange private conduct reporting **without including incident details or personal information**. If immediate safety is at risk, use the relevant platform reporting tools or local emergency services.

Maintainers will limit access to reports, consider the safety and wishes of affected people, and avoid sharing identities without consent where possible. A maintainer named in a report should not handle that report. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban accounts, based on severity and repeated behavior. They will explain a decision to affected parties when safe and practical.
